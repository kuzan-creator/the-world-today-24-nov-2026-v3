One image summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026: 35 stories on a single
newspaper-style page — cream paper, navy masthead, the date as a giant
centred serif headline with a red italic subline beneath it, a numbers
strip, a region colour bar, three hero cards, ten illustrated cards (each
hero/card box filled slightly-white CARD, like the 25 November entry), a
blue-sea world map with real lon/lat polygon land and region-coloured
numbered pins, a numbered list of the remaining 22 stories (every item
on exactly one line, every number in its region's colour), a DATE CHECK
box and a sources footer. Entered in the make.fun bounty "Sum up 24
November's world news in one image":
https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124

## What changed vs v2

v2 scored 0.740828 (rank 123, round 3) and a careful re-check found a
structural problem: **12 of its 35 stories were dated 23 November, not
24 November** — and its DATE CHECK claimed the COP31 collapse and the
700,000 tally were "left out" while cards 9 and 5 were those very
stories. v3 fixes that and more:

- **12 stories swapped out for 24 November stories** (see the list
  below): Riyadh PIF Tower drone strike and the Chen Jining probe join
  as heroes; Huawei's 10-of-12 conviction, the Iowa explosives blast,
  England's 5,258 heat deaths and the Hanoi blast join as cards;
  Gaza meningitis, US tariffs on 14 partners, UK sanctions on the
  Houthis, Lukashenko, Latvia and the claimed 699 drones join the list.
- **DATE CHECK is now true**: every one of the 35 stories happened, or
  was first reported, on 24 November 2026, and none of them also appears
  in the exclusion list. The Hormuz tanker story is framed as an IRGC
  claim ("Iran claims three tankers hit mines") — the separate
  two-tanker incident of Wednesday 25 November is a different event
  and is not mixed in.
- **Smoke can no longer escape its box**: every illustration is rendered
  onto its own layer and pasted masked to its frame (v2 leaked smoke
  7 px above hero 1, 6 px above hero 3 and 4 px above card 6). A
  pixel scan after rendering asserts zero illustration ink in the
  bands above every box.
- **Map caption moved inside the map** (bottom-left, white text on a
  dark pill over empty South Atlantic sea), so the map's top edge now
  aligns with list item 14's row. Map pins were re-measured (closest
  pair 16.2 px after the final nudge pass; every pin stays in its true
  country/sea area, nudges at most 6 px).
- **Numbers strip corrected**: 3 / 40 / 5,258 / 14 / $41bn / 6,803 —
  the Malaysia figure is 6,803 people returned to Myanmar (AP), not
  6,500 Rohingya. Region bar: Europe 11, Middle East 7, Asia-Pacific
  10, Americas 3, Tech & Business 3, Science & Culture 1 (= 35).

## How it is built

`draw.py` (Python + Pillow) renders `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB)
from the story data in the file; `main.ts` (Node 22) copies `entry.png`
byte for byte to the output directory, so two runs are identical. The
code was written from scratch for this entry (clean-room) — no file was
copied from the v2 entry.
